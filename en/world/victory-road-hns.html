Victory Road (Johto)

Johto
Region
Johto
Entrances
Route 26, Route 23, Route 28, Route 22

Note

This gate between Route 22 and Route 26 links Kanto with Johto. It stays closed until you have beaten the Elite Four of Kanto. After that Prof. Oak calls you to his lab in Pallet Town and hands you the GS Ball; only then does the guard let you pass. You take the GS Ball to New Bark Town.
