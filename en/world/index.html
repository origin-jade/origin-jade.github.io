World

The journey leads through three regions: first Kanto, then Johto, then Hoenn. Here you find every place with its wild Pokémon, trainers, items and shops – 190 places in all.

The three regions

Order of the journey

  1. Kanto: you start in Pallet Town and collect the eight Kanto badges. Wild Pokémon and trainers have the lowest levels here.
  2. Johto: after you beat the Elite Four, Prof. Oak calls you to his lab in Pallet Town and hands you the GS Ball. With it the guard behind Route 22 lets you through the gate to Route 26; from there you go on to New Bark Town. The levels continue where Kanto stopped – wild Pokémon and trainers in Johto are much stronger from the start.
  3. Hoenn: after the Johto title you receive the Hoenn Ticket at the lab in Pallet Town. The ship leaves from Vermilion City or Olivine City and takes you to Route 104. In Hoenn wild Pokémon and trainers are stronger again from the start.
  4. Later the ship also goes to Slateport City and Lilycove City once you have visited them, to the Battle Frontier after the Hoenn title and, with the Aurora Ticket, to Birth Island.

What the pages show

Every region page lists its places in travel order, split into towns, routes, caves and special places, with a search field and a filter. Every place page shows:

  • wild Pokémon by area, method and time of day – with levels and rates
  • gifts, static encounters, legendary Pokémon and trades
  • all trainers with their teams, prize money and notes on rematches
  • items to pick up, hidden items, gifts and Berry trees
  • shops with stock and prices, and move tutors
