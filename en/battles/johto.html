Overview

The Gyms in Badge order. The highest level is the one of the first battle.

Gym LeaderLocationHighest levelReward
1. FalknerViolet CityLv. 48TM51 Roost
2. BugsyAzalea TownLv. 51TM89 U-turn

Note

All levels in Johto are fixed. You can take on Gyms 5 to 7 in any order – whoever comes later uses a stronger team. After your Johto title all Leaders and the League wait with new teams, and every Pokémon obeys you.
