How Trainer levels work

Trainers have fixed levels that do not depend on your team. Only rematches follow your progress.

  • Kanto starts at Lv. 5; the Champion's strongest Pokémon is at Lv. 47 in the first title match.
  • Johto continues from there: the Trainers of the story are between Lv. 44 and Lv. 73, what comes after goes up to Lv. 100.
  • Hoenn is the finale: from Lv. 67 to Lv. 100.
  • In Johto and Hoenn, Trainer Pokémon appear in the evolution stage that fits their level. The pages always show the Pokémon that really enters the battle.
  • In phone rematches the strongest Pokémon is 1 to 6 levels below the level the game expects for your progress; in the rematches of the Kanto Gym Leaders 1 to 4 levels above it.
