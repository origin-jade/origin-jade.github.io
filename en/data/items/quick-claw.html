Held by wild Pokémon

Wild Pokémon of these species can hold the item. Normally a wild Pokémon holds its common item with a 45% chance and its rare item with a 10% chance. With a Pokémon that has Compound Eyes or Super Luck at the front of your party it is 60% and 20%.
