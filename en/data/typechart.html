Physical or special by type

The setting “PHYS/SP SPLIT” (tab “MODE” of the challenges) decides where a move gets its category from.

OFF
PHYSICAL and SPECIAL MOVES depend on the Pokémon TYPE.
ON
PHYSICAL and SPECIAL MOVES are MOVE specific.

With “OFF” the category follows the type of the move; status moves stay status moves.
