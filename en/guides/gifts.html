Starters

PokémonPlaceLevelHow to get it
Bulbasaur, Charmander, SquirtlePallet TownLv. 5At the start of the game in Professor Oak's lab: one of three. It is shiny exactly when it was shiny in the Poké Ball's preview.
Chikorita, Cyndaquil, TotodileNew Bark TownLv. 5In Professor Elm's lab, after you bring him the GS Ball from Professor Oak: one of three.
Treecko, Torchic, MudkipLittleroot TownLv. 5In Professor Birch's lab: one of three. Only after the Hoenn title and once you have caught every Pokémon of the Hoenn Pokédex (Completing the Pokédex).

Note

If your party is full, a gift goes to a PC Box. Eggs and the Pokémon with a fixed name need a free slot in your party.
