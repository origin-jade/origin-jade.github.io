Completing the Pokédex

What the game counts as “complete” and what you get for it. Only caught Pokémon count, not Pokémon you have merely seen.

On this page

  1. Rewards for the Pokédex
  2. Diplomas
  3. Professor Oak's aides
  4. Stars on the Trainer Card
  5. Which Pokémon count

Rewards for the Pokédex

Every region has its own list. Once you have caught a whole list, collect the reward in person.

PokédexNeededCollect fromReward
Kanto150 caughtProfessor Oak, Pallet TownExp. Charm
Johto278 caughtProfessor Oak, Pallet TownShiny Charm
Hoenn207 caughtProfessor Oak, Pallet TownAurora Ticket
Hoenn207 caughtProfessor Birch, Littleroot Town (after the Hoenn title)one of the Johto starters Cyndaquil, Totodile or Chikorita (Lv. 5)

Diplomas

A diploma is the certificate for a complete Pokédex. Once you have caught all 415 Pokémon of the National Pokédex, every diploma shows the National Pokédex.

DiplomaNeededWhere
Kanto diploma150 caughtshow it to the game designer in Celadon City
Johto diploma278 caughtshow it to the game designer in Lilycove City

Professor Oak's aides

Aides of Professor Oak wait in Kanto. They reward you once you have caught enough different Pokémon.

PlaceNeededReward
Route 210 caughtHM05
Route 1020 caughtEverstone
Route 1130 caughtDowsing Machine
Route 1640 caughtAmulet Coin
Route 1550 caughtExp. Share

Note

The Kanto Pokédex is counted; if you play with randomly swapped species, the National one counts. If you already have the Exp. Share, you get the Exp. Share S instead.

Stars on the Trainer Card

Each completed goal gives one star, in any order. With all 4 stars the nurse at the Pokémon Center greets you differently.

  • Become Champion of Johto
  • Become Champion of Kanto
  • Become Champion of Hoenn
  • Defeat Red (Mt. Silver)
