Breeding

How breeding works in Origin Jade: how to get an egg, what the baby inherits from its parents and how it hatches.

On this page

  1. Getting an egg
  2. What the baby inherits
  3. Hatching
  4. Odd Egg

Getting an egg

Leave two Pokémon at a Day Care. Whether they produce an egg depends on how well they get along; the game checks every 256 steps.

Day Cares

There is one Day Care per region: Route 5 (Kanto), Route 34 (Johto) and Route 117 (Hoenn). Each Day Care has its own two slots.

Compatibility

What the Day-Care Man says tells you the chance of an egg per check. The Oval Charm raises it.

What the Day Care saysChanceWith Oval Charm

“The two seem to get along.”50%80%

“The two prefer to play with other POKéMON than each other.”0%0%
  • Same species, different Trainers: highest tier.
  • Same species from the same Trainer, or different species from different Trainers: middle tier.
  • Different species from the same Trainer: lowest tier.
  • With Ditto: middle tier, lowest tier if both are from the same Trainer.
  • There is no egg with two Pokémon of the same gender, with genderless partners without Ditto, with two Ditto, without a shared Egg Group and with Pokémon that cannot lay eggs.

Oval Charm

You receive the Oval Charm together with your first egg from a Day Care.

Room in the party

You only get the egg if there is a free slot in your party – this also applies when you play with a smaller party.

Nidoran♂ and Volbeat

A Nidoran♂ or Volbeat with Ditto can also produce a Nidoran♀ or Illumise.

What the baby inherits

Species, nature, IVs, ability, ball and moves – this is how the game passes them on.

Species

The baby is the first evolution stage of the mother; with Ditto that of the other parent.

Incense and baby Pokémon

Baby Pokémon hatch without an incense: no parent has to hold anything for it.

Nature

A parent holding an Everstone always passes on its nature. If both hold one, it is decided at random.

IVs

The baby inherits 3 IVs from its parents, or 5 if a parent holds a Destiny Knot. If a parent holds a Power item (Power Weight, Power Bracer, Power Belt, Power Lens, Power Band and Power Anklet), the baby inherits the matching IV from that parent.

Ability

The baby inherits the mother's ability (with Ditto: the other parent's) – a regular one with a 80% chance, a Hidden Ability with a 60% chance.

Ball

Same species: the ball of a random parent. Otherwise the mother's ball, with Ditto the other parent's. Master Ball, Cherish Ball and Strange Ball become a Poké Ball.

Egg moves

Both parents pass on egg moves. TM moves are not inherited. The egg moves of a species are listed on its page in the Pokédex.

Passing egg moves at the Day Care

Two Pokémon of the same species share their egg moves at the Day Care. With a Mirror Herb this also works between different species.

Special moves

Pichu can learn Volt Tackle if a parent holds a Light Ball.

Hatching

Carry the egg in your party until it hatches.

Hatch level

Pokémon hatch at Lv. 1.

Steps until it hatches

An egg needs as many egg cycles as the species' page in the Pokédex says; one cycle is 256 steps. With Magma Armor, Flame Body or Steam Engine in the party it takes half as long.

Odd Egg

The son of the Day-Care couple at the Day Care on Route 34 gives you an Odd Egg once if you answer his question with the right four words. The books in the Day Care give hints. Your answer decides which Pokémon is in the egg. The egg has a 14% chance to be shiny.
