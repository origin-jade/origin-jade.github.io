Pokéblocks

The Berry Blender turns berries into Pokéblocks. A block's colour depends on its flavors – and it decides what the block does in a Safari Zone feeder.

On this page

  1. Making Pokéblocks
  2. Which colour a block gets
  3. The Safari Zone feeder

Making Pokéblocks

Berry Blenders stand here: Fallarbor Town, Lilycove City, Safari Zone Gate, Slateport City and Verdanturf Town. The flavor of every berry is in the list of berries.

The Safari Zone feeder

  • While a block is in the feeder, the wild Pokémon there have their Hidden Ability if their species has one.
  • The block's colour fixes 3 IVs that are perfect (31). A gold block makes 5 IVs perfect.
  • With a 80% chance the Pokémon has a nature that likes the block's flavor.
  • A block lasts 300 steps. At most 10 feeders can be filled at the same time.
  • The feeder works on the whole map it stands on.
  • Only the colour counts – how well you blended changes nothing.
  • The boards at the Safari Zone entrance (Safari Zone Gate) show the same tables in the game.
