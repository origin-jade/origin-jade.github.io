Level & EXP system

In Origin Jade you play three regions in a row with the same team. To make that work, every opponent has a fixed level that keeps rising from region to region, and experience is weighted differently in each region.

  2. Experience

  5. Obedience
  6. Levels in rematches

Fixed levels

Every wild Pokémon and every Trainer's Pokémon has a fixed level. Nothing depends on your team: train a lot and it gets easier, rush through and it gets harder.

Kanto is the start and runs up to the League. Johto picks up from there and Hoenn goes higher still – you meet a region's highest levels late or after its title. On the way from Kanto to Johto (Route 26, Route 27 and Tohjo Falls) the levels are a little lower than in the rest of Johto.

Gift Pokémon, static encounters and roaming Pokémon have their own hand-set levels; you find them on the pages of those Pokémon and places.

Because the levels in Johto and Hoenn are higher than in the originals, Trainers' Pokémon there usually show up evolved. The Trainer pages list the teams as you meet them.

Experience

Experience depends on the region the battle takes place in. That way your team grows with the journey instead of running away from the opposition.

Distance to the opponent

  • If your Pokémon is more than 4 levels above the defeated or caught Pokémon, it only gets 25% of the value in the table.
  • If it is more than 6 levels below, it gets full experience (100%) – in Kanto only from the third Badge on.

What else counts

  • As in the newer games, the basis is the opponent's base experience times its level: stronger opponents give a weaker Pokémon more, weaker opponents less.
  • Trainer battles give 1.5 times as much.
  • Catching a Pokémon gives experience too.
  • Traded Pokémon get 1.5 times as much.
  • The Lucky Egg and the Exp. Charm each give 1.5 times as much. Prof. Oak gives you the Exp. Charm for completing the Kanto Pokédex.
  • The challenge “Exp. Multiplier” multiplies everything at the end: x1.0 / x1.5 / x2.0 / x0.0.
  • At Lv. 100 there is no more experience, but effort values are still earned.

Exp. Share

The Exp. Share is in your Bag from the start and switched on. Use it in the Bag to turn it off or on again.

While it is on, experience is shared, not multiplied: one half is split among the Pokémon that fought, the other half is spread evenly over every Pokémon in the party that can battle – the fighters included.

While it is off, only the Pokémon that took part get experience. Turn it off if you want to raise a single Pokémon quickly.

Level cap

The level cap is optional: you switch it on in the challenge menu under “Level Cap” (Off / Normal / Hard). Without that challenge there is no cap.

The cap follows your Kanto Badges. At the cap a Pokémon gains no more experience, a Rare Candy has no effect, and it does not grow past it at the Day Care either.

Obedience

Pokémon you caught, hatched or were given always obey, whatever their level. Only Pokémon with a different original Trainer – traded ones, including those from in-game trades – can refuse orders when their level is above the limit.

Up to the title in Kanto the Kanto Badges count, after it the Johto Badges. After the title in Johto every Pokémon obeys.

Levels in rematches

Rematches are the one exception to fixed levels: they grow with your progress. The game does not look at your team for that but at your Badges and titles, and assumes a reference level for them.

In rematches by Pokégear the opponent's strongest Pokémon is 1 to 6 levels below the reference level. The eight Kanto Gym Leaders can be challenged again in their Gyms after the Kanto title; their strongest Pokémon is 1 to 4 levels above it. The other Pokémon of a team keep their distance to the strongest.
