Settings & game modes

Origin Jade has two menus for settings: the options for comfort, battles and sound, and the challenges for the rules of the game – from the movepool to Nuzlocke, the randomizer and the level cap. Every entry is listed here with the text the game shows.

Where do I set what?

You can open the options from the start menu at any time. They have 3 pages, and everything there can be changed as often as you like.

The challenges come up in a new game after you have chosen boy or girl, before you enter your name. L and R turn the 6 pages; after the last page you confirm your choices. Later the same menu is on every PC under “Challenges”.

Once the game has started, many rules can only be relaxed: entries marked “Can only be relaxed later” only let you move to a choice further up their list – back towards the first choice, never further down. So think about those rules before you start.

Options

Comfort, battles and sound. None of these settings change the rules of the game.

Options

Text Speed

Choose one of the four text-display speeds.

1 / 2 / 3 / 4

Battle Scene

  • On – Show the PKMN animations and attack animations.
  • Off – Skip the PKMN animations and attack animations.

Battle Style

  • Shift – Get the option to switch your PKMN after the enemies faints.
  • Set – No free switch after fainting the enemies PKMN.

Button Mode

  • Normal – All buttons work as normal.
  • LR – On some screens the L and R buttons act as left and right.
  • L=A – The L button acts as another A button for one-handed play.

Follower

  • On – Let the first PKMN in your party follow you.
  • Off – Walk alone.

Big Followers

  • On – Enable large PKMN followers. Can cause graphical issues.
  • Off – Disable large PKMN followers. Recommended.

Autorun

  • On – Run without pressing B.
  • Off – Press and hold B to run.

Autorun (Surf)

  • On – Surf faster without pressing B.
  • Off – Press and hold B to surf faster.

Easier Fishing

  • On – Automatically reel while fishing.
  • Off – Manually reel while fishing. Fish like you always fished!

Faster Joy

  • On – NURSE JOY heals you faster.
  • Off – NURSE JOY heals you with the usual animation.

Unit System

  • Metric – Display BERRY and PKMN weight and size in kilograms and meters.
  • Imperial – Display BERRY and PKMN weight and size in pounds and inches.

Match Calls

  • On – TRAINERs will be able to call you, offering rematches and info.
  • Off – You will not receive calls. Special events will still occur.

Battle Options

Fast Intro

  • On – Skip the sliding animation and enter battles faster.
  • Off – Battles load at the usual speed.

Fast Battles

  • On – Skips all delays in battles, which makes them faster.
  • Off – Manual delay skipping. You can press A or B to skip delays.

Battle Terrain

  • Old – Original battle terrain backgrounds.
  • Modern – Modernized battle terrain backgrounds, from HnS.

Battle UI

  • Gen 3 – Original GEN III Battle UI.
  • Gen 4 – Modernized GEN IV Battle UI.

Ball Prompt

  • On – Press R in battle to use Pokeballs. Hold L/R to swap PKMNBALLS.
  • Off – Disables the prompt to use PKMNBALLS quickly.

Quick Run

  • No – No quick running from battles.
  • L+R+A – Hold L+R, then A to run from battles before they start.
  • B→A – Press B to move the cursor to the RUN option after the battle started.
  • B – Press B to run from battles before they start.

Run Prompt

  • On – Enables a prompt to show that you can run away from battles.
  • Off – Disables said prompt to flee. Button combo still works.

Sound

Sound

  • Mono – Sound is the same in all speakers. Recommended for original hardware.
  • Stereo – Play the left and right audio channel separately. Great with headphones.

Music

  • On – Enables music playback. Change maps to take effect.
  • Off – Disables music playback. Change maps to take effect.

Bike Music

  • On – Enables BIKE music.
  • Off – Disables BIKE music.

Surf Music

  • On – Enables SURF music.
  • Off – Disables SURF music.

Challenges: Mode

The basic rules of the game. With “Recommended” the marked settings apply and the other entries of this page are locked; “Custom” lets you change them one by one. Everything here can also be changed freely later.

Gamemode

  • Recommended Default – Recommended settings.
  • Custom – Choose your own rules.

PKMN Movepool

  • Gen 3 – Generation 3 LEARNSETS and EGG MOVES with no changes.
  • Gen 7 Recommended – Generation 7 LEARNSETS and EGG MOVES + minor changes.

Phys/Sp Split

  • Off – PHYSICAL and SPECIAL MOVES depend on the PKMN TYPE.
  • On Recommended – PHYSICAL and SPECIAL MOVES are MOVE specific.

Add Fairy Type

  • Off – FAIRY TYPE isn't added to PKMN that got it in GEN VI.
  • On Recommended – FAIRY TYPE is added / changed to certain PKMN, as in GEN VI.

Reusable TMs

  • Off – TMs are not reusable. Like in the original.
  • On Recommended – TMs are reusable.

Nature Mints

  • Off – Mints are not available ingame until finishing the game.
  • On Recommended – Mints can be bought at the FLOWER SHOP after the 3rd JOHTO badge.

Recharge Moves

  • Gen 3 Recommended – RECHARGE MOVES like HYPER BEAM will always need to recharge after use.
  • Gen 1 – If a RECHARGE MOVE KO's the opponent, no recharge turn is needed.

Synchronize

  • Original – SYNCHRONIZE works as in GEN III. 50% chance to copy nature.
  • Modern Recommended – SYNCHRONIZE works as in GEN VIII+. 100% chance to copy nature.

Sturdy

  • Original – STURDY works as in GEN III. Only negates OHKO moves (GUILLOTINE, etc.)
  • Modern Recommended – STURDY works as in GEN V+. PKMN survive lethal hits with 1HP.

Sitrus Berry

  • Original – SITRUS BERRY restores 30HP. Same as GEN III.
  • Modern Recommended – SITRUS BERRY restores 25% of total HP. Same as GEN IV and up.

Legen. Abilities

  • Off – PRESSURE stays as the main ability of some legendaries.
  • On Recommended – Legendaries have PRESSURE changed for a better ability.

Survive Poison

  • Off – Your PKMN will faint if they are POISONED.
  • On Recommended – Your PKMN will survive the POISON status with 1HP.

Challenges: Features

Clock, shiny odds and small extras. The clock speed can only be set with the simulated clock. If the real clock reports an error when a new game starts, the simulated clock is already selected.

Clock Type

  • RTC Default – Use vanilla Real Time Clock.
  • Fake RTC – Use a fake Real Time Clock. Set its speed under CLOCK SPEED.

Clock Speed

  • Fast Default – 1h in real life = 1 day in-game. BERRIES grow very fast.
  • Normal – 2h in real life = 1 day in-game. BERRIES grow quickly.
  • Slow – 4h in real life = 1 day in-game. BERRIES grow slowly.

Shiny Chance

  • 8192 Default – Very low chance of SHINY encounter. Default chance from Generation III.
  • 4096 – Low chance of SHINY encounter. Default chance from Generation VI+.
  • 2048 – Decent chance of SHINY encounter.
  • 1024 – High chance of SHINY encounter.
  • 512 – Very high chance of SHINY encounter.

Alt Shiny

  • Off Default – Original shiny color palette for all PKMN. Default.
  • On – Some shiny PKMN have brand new color palettes.

Item Drop

  • Off Default – Wild PKMN items will be only obtainable via capture or THIEF.
  • On – Wild PKMN will drop their hold item after defeating them.

Frontier Bans

  • Ban Default – Powerful legendary PKMN are banned in the BATTLE FRONTIER. Default.
  • Unban – All legendaries are allowed to participate in the BATTLE FRONTIER.

Challenges: Randomizer

The randomizer shuffles whatever you switch on; the entries below it can only be chosen while the randomizer is on. Everything on these pages describes the game without the randomizer.

Randomizer

  • Off Default – Game will not be randomized.
  • Random – Play the game randomized. Settings below!

Can only be relaxed later

Starter PKMN

  • Off Default – Standard starter PKMN.
  • Random – Randomize starter PKMN.

Can only be relaxed later

Wild PKMN

  • Off Default – Same wild encounter as in the base game.
  • Random – Randomize wild PKMN.

Can only be relaxed later

Map Seeded

  • Off – Wild encounters are fully random every time.
  • On Default – Wild encounters are seeded per map. Same area always has the same PKMN.

Trainer

  • Off Default – Trainer will have their expected party.
  • Random – Randomize enemy trainer parties.

Can only be relaxed later

Static PKMN

  • Off Default – Static encounters will be the same as in the base game.
  • Random – Named PKMN, casino PKMN, roamers, and some other special PKMN won't change.

Can only be relaxed later

Balancing

  • On Default – PKMN replaced with similar tiered ones. Currently based on evo stages.
  • Off – Distribution of PKMN not balanced around their strength!

Legendaries

  • Off Default – Legendary PKMN will not be included and randomized.
  • On – Include legendary PKMN in randomization!

Can only be relaxed later

Type

  • Off Default – PKMN types stay the same as in the base game.
  • Random – Randomize all PKMN types.

Can only be relaxed later

Moves

  • Off Default – PKMN moves stay the same as in the base game.
  • Random – Randomize all PKMN moves.

Can only be relaxed later

Abilities

  • Off Default – PKMN abilities stay the same as in the base game.
  • Random – Randomize all PKMN abilities.

Can only be relaxed later

Evolutions

  • Off Default – PKMN evolutions stay the same as in the base game.
  • Random – Randomize all PKMN evolutions.

Can only be relaxed later

Evo Lines

  • Off Default – The PKMN that can potentially evolve are unchanged.
  • Random – Randomize evolution lines. Allows new evolution lines to occur!

Can only be relaxed later

Effectiveness

  • Off Default – Type effectiveness chart will remain the same as in the base game.
  • Random – Randomize type effectiveness. WARNING: CAN BE BUGGY!

Can only be relaxed later

Items

  • Off Default – All found or received items are the same as in the base game.
  • Random – Randomize found, hidden and received items. KEY items are excluded!

Can only be relaxed later

Chaos Mode

  • Off Default – Chaos mode disabled.
  • Chaos – Every above chosen option will be very chaotic. NOT recommended!

Can only be relaxed later

Challenges: Nuzlocke

The Nuzlocke rules start as soon as Prof. Oak gives you the Pokédex and end with the first title in Hoenn. On “Easy” the extra rules are fixed; from “Normal” on you can set them one by one.

Nuzlocke

  • Off Default – Nuzlocke mode is disabled.
  • Easy – Fainted PKMN can't be used anymore! No more rules are enforced.
  • Normal – One catch per route! Fainted PKMN can't be used anymore.
  • Hard – Same rules as NORMAL but also deletes SAVE on battle loss!

Can only be relaxed later

Dupes Clause

  • On Default – Only not prior caught PKMN count as first encounter. RECOMMENDED!
  • Off – The player always has to catch the first PKMN per route.

Can only be relaxed later

Shiny Clause

  • On Default – The player can always catch shiny PKMN. RECOMMENDED!
  • Off – The player can only catch a shiny PKMN if it's the first encounter.

Can only be relaxed later

Nicknames

  • On Default – Forces the player to nickname every PKMN. RECOMMENDED!
  • Off – Nicknames are optional.

Can only be relaxed later

Fainting

  • Cemetery Default – Fainted PKMN are sent to the PC after battle and can't be retrieved.
  • Release – Fainted PKMN are released after battle!

Can only be relaxed later

Inf. Rare Candy

  • On – Infinite Rare Candy will be in the player's PC at game start.
  • Off Default – Player will not have access to Infinite Rare Candy.

Only at the start

Challenges: Difficulty

Switches for easier or harder battles. How exactly the level cap works is explained under Level & EXP system.

Party Limit

Limits the amount of PKMN in the party. 1 has visual bugs in DOUBLES.

Off Default / 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 / 1

Can only be relaxed later

Level Cap

  • Off Default – No level cap. Overleveling possible.
  • Normal – Maximum level is based on the next gym's highest PKMN level.
  • Hard – Maximum level is based on the next gym's lowest PKMN level.

Can only be relaxed later

Exp. Multiplier

  • x1.0 Default – PKMN gain normal EXP. Points.
  • x1.5 – PKMN gain 50 percent more EXP. Points!
  • x2.0 – PKMN gain double EXP. Points!
  • x0.0 – PKMN gain ZERO EXP. Points!!!

Player Items

  • Yes Default – The player can use battle items.
  • No – The player can NOT use battle items. Hold items are allowed!

Can only be relaxed later

Trainer Items

  • Yes Default – Enemy trainers can use battle items.
  • No – Enemy trainers can NOT use battle items.

Player IVs

  • Yes Default – Your PKMN have the expected IVs (between 0 and 31).
  • No – The IVs of your PKMN are set always to the maximum (31).
  • No (HP) – IVs are set between 30 and 31 to allow different Hidden Powers.

Trainer IVs

  • Off Default – The PKMN of enemy Trainer have the expected IVs.
  • Scale – The IVs of Trainer PKMN increase with gym badges!
  • Hard – All Trainer PKMN have perfect IVs!

Can only be relaxed later

Player EVs

  • Yes Default – The player's PKMN gain effort values as expected.
  • No – The player's PKMN gain no effort values until the Hoenn League is won!

Can only be relaxed later

Trainer EVs

  • Off Default – The PKMN of enemy Trainer have no EVs.
  • Scale – The EVs of Trainer PKMN increase with gym badges!
  • Hard – All Trainer PKMN have high EVs!
  • Extrem – All Trainer PKMN have 252 EVs! Very Hard!

Can only be relaxed later

Less Escapes

  • Off Default – The player can easily run away from battles, as usual.
  • On – The player can't easily run away from battles. Use repels!

Can only be relaxed later

Esc. Rope / Dig

  • Yes Default – ESCAPE ROPE and DIG can be used to exit dungeons.
  • No – ESCAPE ROPE and DIG can't be used to exit dungeons.

Can only be relaxed later

Challenges: Challenges

Special rules for runs of your own making. “Mirror Thief” only exists together with “Mirror Mode”. If you pick the Fairy type for “One Type Only”, the Fairy type is switched on automatically.

PKMN Center

  • Yes Default – The player can visit PKMNcenters and other locations to heal.
  • No – Healing only with items. No PKMN CENTERS, beds or helpers.

Can only be relaxed later

Ultra Expensive!

  • Off Default – Everything has the usual cost.
  • x5 – Everything is 5 times more expensive!
  • x10 – Everything is 10 times more expensive! Good ol' capitalism.
  • x50! – Everything is 50 times more expensive! Ultra capitalism!

Can only be relaxed later

Evo Limit

  • Off Default – PKMN evolve as expected.
  • First – PKMN can only evolve into their first evolution.
  • All – PKMN can NOT evolve at all!

Can only be relaxed later

One Type Only

20 choices: each of the 18 types, Random (the game draws a type) and Off Default

NormalFightingFlyingPoisonGroundRockBugGhostSteelFireWaterGrassElectricPsychicIceDragonDarkFairy

Allow only one PKMN type the player can capture and use.

BST Equalizer

  • Off Default – All PKMN have their original base stats.
  • 100 – PKMN stats are calculated with 100 of each base stat.
  • 255 – PKMN stats are calculated with 255 of each base stat.
  • 500 – PKMN stats are calculated with 500 of each base stat.

Can only be relaxed later

Mirror Mode

  • Off Default – The player uses their own party.
  • On – In Trainer battles, the player gets a copy of the enemy's party!

Can only be relaxed later

Mirror Thief

  • Off Default – The player gets their own party back after battles.
  • On – The player keeps the enemies party after battle!

Can only be relaxed later

Field moves despite special rules

With Nuzlocke, a party limit, Mirror Mode, a single type, or a randomizer for starters, wild or static Pokémon, moves or evolutions, the first Pokémon in your party may use any field move even if it could not normally learn it. That way you never get stuck.
