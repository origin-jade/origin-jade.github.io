Getting started & FAQ

The quick way into Pokémon Origin Jade: what to expect in your first hour – and answers to the questions that come up most on the way.

The first steps

  1. Choose your rules. First you choose your character on the character wheel: Red, Leaf, Gold, Kris, Brendan or May. After the name the challenge menu comes up. “Recommended” gives you the recommended rules; if you change nothing else, Nuzlocke, the randomizer and every handicap are off. The menu itself explains what the entries mean.
  2. At home. You start in Pallet Town. Mom gives you the Pokégear and the Running Shoes; the Exp. Share (switched on) and the GB Player are already in your Bag.
  3. At the lab. Prof. Oak gives you your first Pokémon and the Pokédex. If you play with Nuzlocke rules, they apply from this moment.
  4. Pick up the radio. In Viridian City the old man who shows you how to catch Pokémon unlocks the radio on your Pokégear.
  5. Set off. From here Kanto runs as you know it: eight Badges, then the Pokémon League. After that you carry on in Johto with the same team.

Good to know

  • You never have to teach an HM: the HM in your Bag and a Pokémon in your party that could learn the move are enough.
  • Opponents have fixed levels. It pays to raise the whole team – the levels keep climbing until the end in Hoenn.
  • You can look at the rules of the challenge menu on any PC, and relax many of them later.

Frequently asked questions

Story and regions

The guard at the Route 22 gate will not let me through to Johto – why?

The way only opens after the title in Kanto. Then Prof. Oak asks you to his lab in Pallet Town and gives you the GS Ball. Only then does the guard in the gate at the end of Route 22 let you through; from there Route 26 leads to New Bark Town, where Prof. Elm takes the GS Ball.

Before the title the guard says: “Hold it! Beyond this gate lies JOHTO. The road stays closed until the ELITE FOUR have been defeated. Prove yourself here first.” After the title, but without the visit to Prof. Oak: “Hold it! PROF. OAK called here. He wants to see you at his lab in PALLET TOWN before you go on to JOHTO.”

I am Champion of Johto but cannot travel to Hoenn – what is missing?

You are missing the Hoenn Ticket. After the title, go to Prof. Oak's lab in Pallet Town: Steven is waiting there and gives it to you. The ship to Hoenn leaves from Vermilion City and Olivine City. The first crossing ends on Route 104; from there you go to Prof. Birch in Littleroot Town.

Without a ticket the sailor says: “You need a ticket for the crossing to HOENN. Without one I can't take you along. Sorry!” Later crossings go straight to Slateport City or Lilycove City once you have been there.

The ships do not sail to Deoxys' island – how do I get there?

You need the Aurora Ticket. Professor Oak gives it to you in his lab in Pallet Town once you have caught every Pokémon of the Hoenn Pokédex. With the ticket in your Bag the ships offer Birth Island as a destination.

The whole way is described under Legendary Pokémon.

Suddenly someone else is in the Gyms of Viridian City and Fuchsia City – what happened?

Both Gyms change their Leader during the story: once you have all eight Johto Badges and the battle against Ho-Oh or Lugia is behind you, your rival takes over the Gym of Giovanni in Viridian City, and Janine that of Koga in Fuchsia City.

You can challenge both as often as you like. There is no Badge, but one TM each: Trick Room from your rival, Poison Jab from Janine.

Where are Sinjoh and the Alola islands from Heart & Soul?

They cannot be visited in Origin Jade because they do not fit the story of the journey through Kanto, Johto and Hoenn.

How do I switch between the regions on the map?

The PokéGear map shows Kanto and Johto together. Once you have been to Hoenn, press Select to switch to the Hoenn map and back – or move further down at the bottom edge, and further up at the top edge of the Hoenn map.

Gameplay

How do rematches work?

Through the Pokégear. Trainers you beat register themselves and call once you have 3 Badges in their region. There is no Vs. Seeker. The Kanto Gym Leaders can be challenged again right in their Gyms after the title in Kanto.

If the calls bother you, turn them off in the options under “Match Calls”.

What does the Exp. Share do?

The Exp. Share is in your Bag, switched on, from the start. While it is on, half of the experience goes to the Pokémon that fought and the other half is spread over the whole party. So experience is shared, not doubled. Use it in the Bag to turn it off or on.

Do I have to teach HM moves?

No. The HM in your Bag and a Pokémon in your party that could learn the move are enough. You still need the region's go-ahead: the Badge in Kanto, and in Johto and Hoenn the permission for that move.

With some challenges – Nuzlocke, a party limit or a single type, for example – the first Pokémon in your party may use the field move even if it cannot learn it.

Where do I get the Pokédex rewards?

From Prof. Oak at his lab in Pallet Town. Talk to him when a regional Pokédex is complete: Kanto (150 Pokémon): Exp. Charm; Johto (278 Pokémon): Shiny Charm; Hoenn (207 Pokémon): Aurora Ticket. Caught Pokémon are what counts.

Which Pokémon belong to each is listed under Completing the Pokédex.

Pokémon

I knocked out a legendary Pokémon – is it gone?

No. Legendary Pokémon you knocked out come back after every League victory, ones that fled after 3 changes of day. Only caught ones never return.

Where to find them is listed under Legendary Pokémon.

Suicune keeps running away from me – where can I catch it?

That is part of the story: you meet Suicune several times before it stays to battle. You can only catch it in the Tin Tower (ground floor), after you have defeated the Kimono Girls.

The whole way is described under Legendary Pokémon.

Where do I get Mints to change a nature?

That depends on the setting “Nature Mints” in the challenge menu:

  • Off – Mints are not available ingame until finishing the game.
  • On – Mints can be bought at the FLOWER SHOP after the 3rd JOHTO badge.

Rules and clock

Can I still change the rules after starting?

Yes, on any PC under “Challenges”. The basic rules and the settings for the clock and the shiny odds can be changed freely. Nuzlocke, the randomizer, difficulty and special rules can almost all only be relaxed, not tightened.

The clock does not run properly – what can I do?

Switch “Clock Type” in the challenge menu to “Fake RTC”; that also works later on any PC. The simulated clock only runs while you play. You choose its speed under “Clock Speed”:

  • Fast – 1h in real life = 1 day in-game. BERRIES grow very fast.
  • Normal – 2h in real life = 1 day in-game. BERRIES grow quickly.
  • Slow – 4h in real life = 1 day in-game. BERRIES grow slowly.

If the real clock reports an error when a new game starts, the game selects the simulated clock by itself.

When do the Nuzlocke rules start and end?

They start as soon as Prof. Oak gives you the Pokédex and end with the first title in Hoenn. The levels in the game:

  • Off – Nuzlocke mode is disabled.
  • Easy – Fainted PKMN can't be used anymore! No more rules are enforced.
  • Normal – One catch per route! Fainted PKMN can't be used anymore.
  • Hard – Same rules as NORMAL but also deletes SAVE on battle loss!
Are these pages right with the randomizer on?

No. All pages describe the game without the randomizer. Whatever you have shuffled differs from them.
