Cacturne

GrassDark Available from Hoenn

After spending thousands of years in harsh deserts, its blood transformed into the same substances as sand. It is nocturnal, so it hunts at night.

Where to find it

Note

Cacturne is available in Hoenn at the earliest. There is no source in Kanto and Johto.

Cacturne does not appear in the wild. You get it by evolving Cacnea.
