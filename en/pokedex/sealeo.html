Sealeo

IceWater Available from Hoenn

Sealeo live in herds on ice floes. Using its powerful flippers, it shatters ice. It dives into the sea to hunt prey five times a day.

Where to find it

Note

Sealeo is available in Hoenn at the earliest. There is no source in Kanto and Johto.

Sealeo does not appear in the wild. You get it by evolving Spheal.
