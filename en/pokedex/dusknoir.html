Dusknoir

Ghost Available from Hoenn

This feared Pokémon is said to travel to worlds unknown. Some even believe that it takes lost spirits into its pliant body and guides them home.

Where to find it

Note

Dusknoir is available in Hoenn at the earliest. There is no source in Kanto and Johto.

Dusknoir does not appear in the wild. You get it by evolving Dusclops.
